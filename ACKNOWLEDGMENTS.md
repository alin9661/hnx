# Acknowledgments

`hnx` is a clean implementation informed by the product ideas in:

- [donnemartin/haxor-news](https://github.com/donnemartin/haxor-news), Apache-2.0
- [danfry1/hacker-news-tui](https://github.com/danfry1/hacker-news-tui), MIT
- [aome510/hackernews-TUI](https://github.com/aome510/hackernews-TUI), MIT

No source code from these projects is copied into `hnx`. Their workflows and
public behavior were used as research inputs: terminal-native reading and
filters from haxor-news, modern asynchronous TUI interaction patterns from
hacker-news-tui, and compact terminal information hierarchy from
hackernews-TUI.

`hnx` is an independent Hacker News client and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Y Combinator.
