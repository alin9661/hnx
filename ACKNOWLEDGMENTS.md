# Acknowledgments

`hnx` is a clean implementation informed by the product ideas in:

- [donnemartin/haxor-news](https://github.com/donnemartin/haxor-news), Apache-2.0
- [danfry1/hacker-news-tui](https://github.com/danfry1/hacker-news-tui), MIT

No source code from either project is copied into `hnx`. Their workflows and public behavior were used as research inputs: terminal-native reading and filters from haxor-news, and modern asynchronous TUI interaction patterns from hacker-news-tui.

`hnx` is an independent Hacker News client and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Y Combinator.
